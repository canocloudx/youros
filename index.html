YOUR-OS

One board, called NOW: what is urgent, what builds your life, what to hand off, and everything still unsorted.

How do I add a task?

Press + at the top of NOW. Type the task and pick its corner of the board; left alone, it goes to Inbox.

How do I start a focus sprint?

Tap a task. Choose how long, write what done looks like and a few small steps, then start the timer. Sprints run by day: one starts after 05:00 and has to end by 22:00.

How do I set a reminder or an alarm?

Swipe a task to the left and tap Remind, or press and hold it and choose Remind me…. Pick a day and a time. Turn on Alarm if it should ring until you stop it, even in silent mode.

How do I give a task to someone?

Press and hold a task and choose Page someone…. Type your name, then send the link any way you like. They open it in YOUR-OS and accept it onto their board. If they do not have YOUR-OS yet, the link shows the task and where to get the app; after installing, they open the same link again.

What are Unload and the Empath?

Unload (top left) lets you write down everything on your mind and sorts it onto the board. Empath (top right) is a thirty-second check-in and a place to take a brain break.
