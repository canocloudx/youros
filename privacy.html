Privacy

YOUR-OS for iPhone · Last updated 19 November 2026

The short version

YOUR-OS keeps your tasks, goals, routines, reminders, focus sprints and Empath check-ins on your iPhone. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not receive your data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your board: tasks, due dates, repeat rules, goals, projects and weekly routines.
  • Reminders and alarms: scheduled on your iPhone as local notifications and system alarms. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Unload your mind: what you type is sorted on your iPhone. It is not sent to a server.
  • The Empath: your check-in answers and the meter built from them are stored in a protected file on your iPhone, excluded from backups. It is a mirror, not a medical test.
  • Focus sprints: their history and the Live Activity shown on your Lock Screen.

The Pager: tasks you send to someone

When you page a task to someone, YOUR-OS creates a link that contains the task's title, its due date if it has one, and the name you typed. You choose how to send that link (Messages, WhatsApp, mail and so on), and whoever has the link can read what is in it. The task rides in the part of the link that browsers do not send to web servers, so the page the link opens does not receive or store it. An answer (accepted or declined) travels back the same way, as a link the other person chooses to send.

Optional two-way sync

Sync is off unless you turn it on in Settings and enter the address and key of a board you run yourself. When it is on, your tasks are sent to that address over HTTPS. We do not operate a sync service for you and do not receive this data.

Permissions

  • Notifications: asked the first time you set a reminder or a focus sprint, to deliver them.
  • Alarms: asked the first time you turn a reminder into an alarm.

You can change either in the Settings app at any time.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone.
